The world of retail broker affiliate schemes is incredibly dirty.

Standard schemes are built on "rebates." The broker pays the affiliate a percentage of the spread every time their referred user takes a trade. 

This creates a massive conflict of interest. **The affiliate is incentivized to make you trade as much as possible, regardless of whether those trades are profitable.** 

They want you to over-leverage, scalp frantically, and click "buy" and "sell" 50 times a day so they can collect their rebates. They profit directly from your transaction volume, which is the fastest way to blow a retail account.

At Drawdown Trading, we refuse to participate in this. That is why we built a partner system based on **pure subscription revenue share**, and launched our newly updated **Affiliate Management Dashboard**.

## 1. Transparency Over Rebates

Our partner program is designed strictly to reward trading educators and developers who teach sustainable risk-management. 

We do not pay rebates on transaction volume. Instead, we pay a flat, recurring **30% commission on platform subscription revenue**.

```

OUR PARTNER MODEL    ──► Incentivizes Longevity    ──► Sustainable Community Growth
```

This simple shift aligns the incentives of the partner with the longevity of the trader. When your referred users learn to manage their drawdowns properly and survive in the markets, they retain their Drawdown Trading subscriptions. You get paid a steady, recurring income, and they protect their capital.

## 3. Strict FCA Compliance for Financial Promotions

UK regulatory standards regarding financial promotion are exceptionally high. Under FCA guidelines, affiliates must never make misleading claims, promise guaranteed returns, or encourage retail traders to risk capital they cannot afford to lose.
