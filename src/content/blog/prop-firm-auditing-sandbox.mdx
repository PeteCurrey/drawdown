"Get funded with £100,000 in 24 hours," they say. "Keep 90% of your profits."

## 1. The Hidden Trap of Consistency Rules

The most common reason successful retail traders get disqualified during a prop evaluation is the **Consistency Rule**.

Imagine you need to make £10,000 of profit to pass. On day 2, you catch a massive GBP/USD news move and make £8,500 on a single trade. You think you are a genius.

But under many firms' consistency parameters, no single trading day can account for more than **30% or 40% of your total profit target**. 

```
YOUR PROFIT LOG:   Day 1: £500 | Day 2: £8,500 (News Run) | Day 3: £500 | Day 4: £500 ──► Total: £10,000
PROP FIRM RULING:  Single Trade (>30% limit) Disqualified! Account Flagged. Reset Fee Required.
```

They do this because they want to filter out gamblers who get lucky on a single high-impact economic print. They want to see **reproducible, systematic execution**. 

Our Auditing Sandbox tracks your rolling equity and automatically alerts you if your profit concentration is shifting too far into a single trade, allowing you to adapt your position sizing and protect your status.

## 2. Real-Time Floating Equity Monitoring

Most prop firm dashboards only calculate your daily drawdown based on your **closed account balance at the end of the day**. 

But their risk engine monitors your **floating equity in real-time**.

If you enter a trade, and it goes into £5,100 of temporary negative float on a £100k account before returning to a profit, you have breached the 5% daily loss limit. Your account is closed immediately, even though your closed balance never showed a loss.

Our Auditing Sandbox solves this by connecting directly to your MT4/MT5 terminal via high-speed APIs. It calculates your **real-time maximum floating drawdown** and maps it against a simulated daily boundary, alerting you with high-contrast screen warnings long before your positions can trigger an automatic liquidation.
